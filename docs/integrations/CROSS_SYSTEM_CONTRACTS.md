# Cross-System Contracts

This is the current-source contract registry for High Value platform handoffs. It preserves existing product boundaries: Atlas is the internal CRM/operating system; EVA, 360 Growth, Agent Copilot, and GCC remain separate systems.

## Authoritative identity anchors

| Entity | Current authority | Stable key | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Company / account | Atlas `HVCG_Clients` | `ClientCode` | Master for prospects and active clients. `ClientStage` is lifecycle state. |
| Contact | Atlas `HVCG_Contacts` | SharePoint item ID plus email | Linked to `HVCG_Clients` where available. |
| Lead | Atlas `HVCG_Leads` | SharePoint item ID plus `HVCG_IdempotencyKey` | Pre-client; may not have `ClientCode`. |
| Opportunity | Atlas `HVCG_Opportunities` | SharePoint item ID plus `HVCG_IdempotencyKey` | Lead conversion key: `opp-from-lead|{LeadId}`. |
| Capital opportunity | Atlas capital module | Capital opportunity ID / `cap-from-opp|{OpportunityId}` where bridged | Does not auto-submit externally. |
| GCC tenant | GCC Supabase | `organizations.id` | No Atlas bridge exists in current scope. |

## Contract rules

1. Do not turn a handoff into a second CRM, second opportunity model, or parallel client database.
2. Do not infer verified financial, legal, credit, tax, or capital facts from AI assessments or marketing attribution.
3. Do not create external commitments silently: no client email, proposal, lender submission, agreement, access provisioning, ad spend, guarantee, money movement, or legal/tax/credit decision.
4. Preserve attribution fields when supplied; never invent missing history.
5. Replays must be idempotent and must not duplicate clients/opportunities/handoffs.
6. `Won` opportunities do not activate clients. Client activation is a separate governed event.
7. GCC financial intelligence stays in GCC; Atlas may link to a governed GCC tenant only after an explicit bridge contract exists.
