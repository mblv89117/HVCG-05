# CONTENT_PLAN

**Brand:** High Value Capital Group LLC  
**Environment:** Staging only  
**As of:** 2026-07-15  
**Sources:** `BRAND_POSITIONING.md`, `VALUE_PROPOSITION.md`, `IDEAL_CLIENT_PROFILE.md`, `SITEMAP.md`, `WIREFRAMES.md`

## Content principles

1. Brand-first Home; one job per page.  
2. Assessment-led CTAs (EVA → Readiness → Call).  
3. No client names, logos, testimonials, or financing results.  
4. No dollar rates until `BL-P1`; describe packaging drivers instead.  
5. Always link Disclaimer on assessment and pricing pages.  
6. Tone: precise, calm, executive (`BRAND_POSITIONING.md`).

## Assessment question banks (content outline)

### EVA (lead-gen)

Contact · company · role · decision-maker Y/N · revenue band · capital intent · timeline · self-rated books quality · top value-driver themes (multi-select) · open challenge (short text)

### Capital Readiness

Capital type · amount band · timeline · collateral themes · existing debt themes · document maturity · prior capital conversations (Y/N, no names) · readiness self-score

**Copy rule:** Every results screen: “Not a financing decision or appraisal.”
