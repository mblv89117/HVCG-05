# IDEAL_CLIENT_PROFILE

**Brand:** High Value Capital Group LLC  
**As of:** 2026-07-15  
**Use:** Messaging, EVA qualification, and disqualification rules (staging)

## Ideal client (summary)

**Privately held operating companies** (roughly SMB to lower-middle-market) whose owners need **capital readiness, finance leadership, or growth-finance discipline**—and who will engage through assessment → call → paid proposal—not free endless advice.

## Firmographic fit

| Attribute | Ideal | Stretch | Poor fit |
|-----------|-------|---------|----------|
| Entity | Operating LLC/Corp with real revenue | Early revenue with strong pipeline | Idea-only / no operations |
| Ownership | Founder/owner or small leadership team | PE-backed needing readiness support | Public co needing IR shop |
| Capital intent | Raise, refinance, restructure, or prepare | Exploring options seriously | “Just curious” with no timeline |
| Finance maturity | Books exist; may be messy | Partial books + willingness to clean | Refuses any financial transparency |
| Decision maker | Owner / CEO / CFO engaged | Ops lead with owner access | Interns / no budget authority |
| Geography | US (primary) | Other with US nexus | Jurisdiction we cannot support |
| Contract entity | Willing to engage **HVCG** as new client | — | Expects legacy HVS pricing without status |

## Psychographic / behavioral fit

- Prefers **clarity and process** over pitch theater  
- Accepts that **readiness work precedes capital conversations**  
- Will complete EVA / document requests in reasonable time  
- Understands HVCG is **advisory**, not a lender or guarantor  

## Buying triggers

- Preparing a raise or refinance in the next 3–12 months  
- Lender/investor feedback: “numbers aren’t clear”  
- Owner wearing CFO hat past capacity  
- Growth plan outrunning financial controls  
- Need for packaged capital narrative and data room hygiene  

## Disqualify / nurture-only (site should soft-gate)

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| No decision maker reachable | Nurture; no strategy call |
| Unwilling to share basic financials | Stop at educational content |
| Seeks guaranteed funding / “intro to my lender” only | FAQ + disclaimer; no proposal path |
| Sole need is tax filing / bookkeeping | Refer-out language (generic); not HVCG core |
| Hostile to assessment process | Do not book call |
| Legacy HVS client seeking reprice via website | Route internal—not new HVCG funnel |

## Persona sketches (anonymous)

### P1 — Capital-seeking operator
Owner of a growing company preparing a capital ask; needs readiness score, narrative, and advisory retainer options.

### P2 — Overloaded founder-CFO
Revenue is real; reporting is late and inconsistent; wants fractional CFO rhythm before or alongside growth.

### P3 — Growth with finance lag
Strong sales motion; ops and finance systems lag; needs business-growth + finance alignment so capital capacity keeps up.

## Qualification score inputs (for funnel team)

Suggested staging fields (no live scoring engine required yet):

- Capital timeline (0–90 / 90–180 / 180+ days)  
- Approximate revenue band (bands only)  
- Books quality self-rating  
- Capital amount range (bands)  
- Service interest (Advisory / FCFO / Growth)  
- Decision-maker confirmed (Y/N)  

## Messaging implications

- Speak to **owners and finance leads**, not generic startups.  
- Lead with **readiness and clarity**, not “we fund you.”  
- Keep **Case Studies** as placeholder until anonymized proofs exist.
