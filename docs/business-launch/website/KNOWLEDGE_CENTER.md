**Brand:** High Value Capital Group LLC

---

## Purpose

Centralize **nurture and education** content separate from conversion pages. Knowledge Center feeds EVA funnel without replacing assessment-led CTAs.

| Section | Path | Phase | Job |

---

## Content pillars (all channels)

1. **Capital readiness** — Preparation before the ask  
2. **Finance operating rhythm** — Fractional CFO themes  
3. **Funding paths** — Educational tradeoffs (no lender endorsements)  
4. **Enterprise value** — Drivers and gaps (not official appraisal)  
5. **Process transparency** — How advisory works

---

## CTA hierarchy (every KC page)

1. Start free EVA (primary)  
2. Capital Readiness (secondary)  
3. Newsletter subscribe (tertiary)  
4. Book Appointment (qualified only — soft mention)
