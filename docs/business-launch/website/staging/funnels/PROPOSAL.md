# Proposal Funnel — Staging Spec

**Scope:** Post–strategy-call proposal path (CRM-owned)  
**Overview page:** `/funnels/proposal` · `staging/funnels/proposal.html`  
**As of:** 2026-07-15

---

## Purpose

Define how qualified leads become signed engagements. The public site explains the path; **proposals are created in HVCG OS**, not generated by the website.

---

## Entry requirements

| Prerequisite | Preferred | Exception |
|--------------|-----------|-----------|
| EVA complete | Yes | Manual qualify with reason |
| Capital Readiness complete | Yes | Owner-approved skip |
| Strategy call held | Yes | — |
| Qualification score | Qualified | Nurture holds |

---

## Proposal stages

| # | Stage | System | Notes |
|---|-------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Discovery call complete | `HVCG_DiscoveryCalls` | Notes + fit decision |
| 2 | Opportunity created | `HVCG_Opportunities` | Service path mapped |
| 3 | Scope & packaging | Internal | Map to SKU (Section B) |
| 4 | **Owner approval** | Required | Final price before send |
| 5 | Proposal artifact | `HVCG_Proposals` | PDF / DocuSign path TBD |
| 6 | Client review | CRM stage | — |
| 7 | Signature + payment | CRM / billing | Triggers onboarding |

---

## Pricing rules (Section B)

| SKU | Setup | Monthly |
|-----|-------|---------|
| SKU-FRA Funding Readiness | FREE | — |
| SKU-CAP-CORE | $5,000 | $3,500 |
| SKU-CAP-GROWTH | $10,000 | $7,500 |
| SKU-CAP-ENT | From $20,000 | From $12,500 |
| Success fees | Debt 1.5% · Equity 3% | — |
| Hourly | Assoc $250 · Senior $350 · Principal $500 | — |

**Final engagement price requires owner approval** before signature.

## Website messaging

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---------|-----------|

## Disqualify paths

- Nurture / disqualified leads → Resources, FAQ — no proposal  
- Legacy HVS reprice requests → internal route, not web funnel

## Copy stub (overview page)
