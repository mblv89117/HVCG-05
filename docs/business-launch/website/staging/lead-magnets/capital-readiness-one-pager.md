### Block 1 — What capital readiness means

Three sentences: readiness = aligned story, numbers, and ask **before** lender/investor conversations. HVCG measures gaps; does not approve funding.

---

### Block 2 — Readiness dimensions (score bands)

| Dimension | Low | Mid | High |
|-----------|-----|-----|------|
| Financial transparency | Books incomplete | Partial, improving | Current, auditable trail |
| Capital narrative | Unclear ask | Draft narrative | Coherent use-of-funds |
| Document maturity | Scattered | Indexed, gaps | Data-room ready |
| Debt structure | Unknown / stressed | Documented | Manageable vs ask |
| Leadership bandwidth | No finance owner | Founder-CFO overload | Fractional/exec rhythm |

**Note:** Bands are illustrative — actual scoring via Capital Readiness Assessment.

---

### Block 3 — Typical gaps (generic)

- Late or inconsistent monthly close  
- No debt schedule or covenant map  
- Use-of-funds not tied to operating plan  
- Customer concentration unaddressed in narrative  
- Personal guarantees not modeled
