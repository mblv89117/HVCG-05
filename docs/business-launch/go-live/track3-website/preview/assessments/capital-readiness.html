Capital Readiness Assessment

Know what is missing before you ask. A readiness score and gap themes—not a financing approval.

Inputs

  • Capital type and amount band
  • Timeline and collateral themes
  • Existing debt themes and document maturity
  • Prior capital conversations (yes/no — no institution names required)
  • Readiness self-score

Output bands (concept)

  • Prepared — Gaps are narrow; strategy call recommended
  • Developing — Specific gaps identified; service path suggested
  • Early — Foundational work needed; Resources and EVA themes referenced
