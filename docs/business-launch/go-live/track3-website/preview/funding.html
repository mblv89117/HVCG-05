Funding paths and tradeoffs

Understand common capital paths before you ask — HVCG educates and prepares; we do not guarantee approval or endorse specific lenders or investors.

Common paths (educational)

  • Debt / refinance — Requires document maturity, debt schedule clarity, and collateral awareness
  • Equity / growth capital — Requires coherent narrative, use-of-funds, and value-driver story
  • Hybrid / structured — Blended instruments; complexity drives advisory scope
  • Internal / organic — Sometimes the right answer is readiness work before external capital
