Frequently asked questions

Are you a lender or broker that guarantees funding?

No. HVCG is a capital advisory and fractional CFO partner. We prepare operators for capital conversations; we do not guarantee financing or approvals.

Do you guarantee valuations or investment returns?

No. Assessments provide structured clarity—not official appraisals or performance promises. See our Disclaimer.

What is the Enterprise Value Assessment?

A free structured intake that identifies value drivers, gaps, and a recommended service path. It takes roughly 10–15 minutes and leads to Capital Readiness when appropriate.

Why Capital Readiness before a strategy call?

Readiness scoring filters fit and educates serious buyers. It ensures the strategy call covers scope and capital intent—not generic discovery.

How does pricing work for new HVCG clients?

New clients use the owner-approved HVCG rate card on our Pricing page. Displayed amounts are framework rates; final engagement price requires owner approval before signature.

Are existing High Value Solution engagements affected by this site?

No — preserved separately. Legacy HVS clients keep contracted pricing. The HVCG rate card applies only to new HVCG prospects and clients unless owner re-contracts.

When is the client portal available?

The client portal is a placeholder on staging. Authenticated portal access ships with the portal module—not this marketing site.

How do I send documents securely?

Use Secure Upload (staging stub). Live guest upload wiring is gated on security review (BL-C1) and platform setup (BL-W1).
