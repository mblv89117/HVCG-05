# KPI, Executive Dashboard, Forecasting, Pipeline Health, and Analytics

## Metric governance

Each metric has an ID, formula, grain, owner, source, refresh cadence, and data
quality threshold. A dashboard must display its `as_of` timestamp and source
watermark. Missing data is unknown, never zero.

## Executive Revenue Dashboard

### Executive strip

| KPI | Formula |
|---|---|
| New leads | count leads created in period |
| Qualified leads | count manually qualified in period |
| Open pipeline | sum open opportunity amount |
| Weighted pipeline | sum amount × probability |
| Commit forecast | sum forecast category Commit + Closed |
| New booked revenue | signed contract value in period |
| MRR | active contracted recurring monthly revenue |
| Collected cash | Finance-reported payments in period |
| Open A/R | invoice amount − collected |
| Past-due A/R | open balance where due date < as-of |

### Funnel panel

- lead → contacted conversion
- contacted → discovery conversion
- discovery → qualified conversion
- qualified → opportunity conversion
- opportunity → proposal conversion
- proposal → signed conversion
- signed → active onboarding conversion
- median time in each stage

### Revenue panel

- booked vs invoiced vs collected
- MRR bridge: opening + new + expansion − contraction − churn
- setup/fixed/success fee by month
- client and service concentration
- renewal pipeline

### Risk panel

- stale leads/opportunities
- opportunities with no next action
- slipped expected close dates
- proposals near expiry
- onboarding blocked
- retainers at risk / renewal overdue
- past-due aging
- missing owner, value, close date, or source

## Forecasting

### Forecast categories

- **Pipeline:** early/open, not management committed
- **Best Case:** plausible within period with identified path
- **Commit:** owner commits to close in period with evidence
- **Closed:** signed/booked fact

### Weighted forecast

`weighted_amount = amount × probability / 100`

Default stage probabilities are starting priors only. They must be calibrated
quarterly using observed conversion rates. Manual probability may not exceed the
stage ceiling without an approval reason.

### Revenue timing

Forecast lines are generated by revenue type:

- setup: expected invoice/contract start month
- MRR: each active month in term
- fixed/milestone: approved milestone month
- success fee: excluded from Commit until trigger evidence; Best Case otherwise
- renewal: separate renewal opportunity until signed

### Forecast scenarios

| Scenario | Included |
|---|---|
| Conservative | Closed + Commit × confidence haircut |
| Base | Closed + Commit + weighted Best Case |
| Upside | Base + weighted Pipeline |

No scenario treats unsigned contract value as booked revenue.

### Forecast accuracy

- absolute error = `|forecast − actual|`
- percentage error = `absolute error / max(actual, materiality floor)`
- bias = `(forecast − actual) / max(actual, materiality floor)`

Track by month, owner, service, category, and forecast vintage.

## Pipeline health

Each open opportunity receives 0–100 health:

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Stage evidence complete | 25 |
| Next action exists and not overdue | 20 |
| Meaningful contact recency | 15 |
| Decision maker/authority identified | 10 |
| Close date credibility | 10 |
| Amount/pricing quality | 10 |
| Data completeness | 10 |

Penalties:

- next action overdue: −20
- stage age above threshold: −15
- close date slipped twice: −15
- no activity 14 days: −15
- proposal expired: −20
- owner missing: −25

Bands:

- Green: 75–100
- Yellow: 50–74
- Red: 0–49

Health is operational guidance, not a substitute for owner judgment.

## Revenue analytics

Required slices:

- time: day/week/month/quarter/year/vintage
- source/campaign/referral
- owner/team
- service/SKU/pricing model
- industry/company size/geography
- stage/status/forecast category
- new vs expansion vs renewal
- client/engagement/project
- invoice type/aging bucket

Required analyses:

- cohort conversion and sales cycle
- source quality and economics
- stage leakage
- win/loss reasons
- pricing exception outcomes
- forecast accuracy and bias
- retention/churn/expansion
- revenue concentration
- invoice aging and collection patterns
- lifecycle data-quality trend

## Alerts

Alerts are internal and mocked:

- high-value hot lead unassigned
- lead response SLA exceeded
- opportunity lacks next action
- close date slipped
- proposal approval/expiry due
- contract unsigned near target start
- onboarding blocked
- renewal notice window open
- invoice past due / aging escalated
- forecast coverage below threshold
- data quality below threshold
