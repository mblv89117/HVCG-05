# Manny Decision Policy (Phase 5A)

Allowed local decisions:

1. Qualified for Consultation  
2. Needs More Information  
3. Not a Fit  
4. Hold for Later  
5. Duplicate  
6. Return AI Review for Revision  
7. Archive Synthetic Record  

These decisions update **local** synthetic prospect/submission status only.

They must **not**: send email · schedule meeting · create proposal · create Production lead · convert client · activate workflow.
