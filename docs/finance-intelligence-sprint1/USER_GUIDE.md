# User Guide — Finance Intelligence

## Who this is for

Owners, executives, and finance leads who need a single place to see performance, cash, working capital, forecasts, risks, and indicative enterprise value.

## Getting started

1. Open the app (local: port 5176).
2. Choose your **Dashboard role** (demo control).
3. Choose **Organization**: HVCG, CCB, or Client aggregate.
4. Start on **Overview**, then drill into Trends, Cash, Working Capital, Budget, Forecast, or Enterprise Value.

## Reading a KPI card

Each card shows current value, period, prior comparison, trend, source, last refresh, status, quality, and a drill-down link.

- **Mock demo** = illustrative HVCG figures from Finance Ops mock data  
- **Awaiting verified data / Data connection pending / Not yet calculated** = incomplete — do not treat as real client financials  

## Colorado Craft Beef

Relationship and capital objectives are shown. Financial metrics stay incomplete until verified financial statements are bound into approved Atlas sources.

## Enterprise value

Estimates are labeled **indicative** unless formally validated. Do not present them as appraisal conclusions.

## AI observations

AI cards are hypotheses for human review. They are never verified accounting conclusions.
